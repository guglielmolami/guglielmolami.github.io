Research

I study how quantum chaos and classical hardness emerge dynamically in quantum systems, both in idealized settings and in the presence of noise.

  • Probing chaos and complexity
    How these features can be detected and characterized, from the statistics of output bitstring probabilities to quantum resources such as entanglement, magic and non-Gaussianity.
  • Exactly solvable models
    Random quantum circuits and other models where these quantities can be computed analytically.
  • Numerical methods
    Tools for simulating quantum many-body systems, in particular tensor networks and Pauli propagation.
