Guglielmo Lami

Researcher in quantum physics

My research explores how quantum chaos and classical hardness emerge dynamically in quantum systems, both in ideal settings and in the presence of noise. I study how these features can be probed and characterized, from the statistics of output bitstring probabilities, to quantum resources such as entanglement, magic, and non-Gaussianity. I focus especially on exactly solvable models, notably random quantum circuits, where these quantities can be computed analytically. I am also interested in developing numerical methods for simulating quantum many-body systems, in particular Tensor Networks and Pauli propagation.
